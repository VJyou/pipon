p!pon Privacy Policy

Covers the p!pon iPhone app and the p!pon demo site. Last updated: 8 October 2026.

p!pon lets you prove a fact about yourself, such as "age 20 or over", from the IC chip of your My Number Card without handing over the data on the card. The proof is made on your iPhone. This page lists everything that leaves your phone and what the demo site keeps.

What stays on your phone

  • The certificate read from your My Number Card, which holds your name, date of birth, sex and address. It is used in memory to build the proof and is never sent, stored or logged.
  • The card's signing password. It is sent only to the card over NFC, as the card requires, and is never stored.
  • Your Individual Number (My Number). The app never reads it.

What the app sends

Only when you approve a request, and only to the site that made the request (the address in the QR code):

DataWhy
A zero-knowledge proofLets the site check the facts below without seeing your card data.
Age threshold met (for example 20+), and today's dateThe fact the site asked for.
Your prefecture code (for example 13 = Tokyo)Some services are limited to a region.
A pseudonymous ID for that serviceLets the service see that the same person does not claim twice. It is different for every service, so services cannot link you with it.
A hash of the issuing authority's key, and a random numberShows the card was issued by a trusted authority, and ties the proof to this one request.

Demo card

If you choose "Demo card", the app sends a random ID made on your phone to the demo site, which issues a dummy card for it: a fictional person and a test key. No real data is involved. The demo site keeps the test key for that random ID so that the same demo card is issued again.

What the demo site keeps

  • Requests and their proofs, in memory, for up to 10 minutes.
  • Your IP address, in memory, for up to one hour, to limit how many proofs one address can request.
  • Demo card test keys, as described above.

There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Blockchain (Web3 demo only)

If you claim on a Solana gate, the proof, your wallet address, the pseudonymous ID for that gate and the public facts are recorded on Solana devnet. Blockchain records are public and cannot be deleted.

Permissions

  • NFC: to read your My Number Card when you tap it.
  • Local network: only when a demo site runs on your own network.

Children

p!pon is a demo and is not directed at children.
