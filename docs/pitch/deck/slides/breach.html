A close-up from Japan. Two weeks ago, one of Japan's largest car-sharing services was breached: about 6.6 million member accounts, and 1.6 million images of driver's licences and other ID documents that members had uploaded to sign up. Police are now reissuing licences. And it is not an isolated case: in 2025, listed companies in Japan alone reported leaks affecting over 30 million people.
