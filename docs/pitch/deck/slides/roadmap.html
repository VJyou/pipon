Our roadmap. Now: a working sample on the My Number Card — age, prefecture and one per person — verified on Solana devnet and in the browser, proven on an iPhone and tested on a real card. Next, by early 2027: the iPhone app in TestFlight and then Android, a drop-in checkout SDK for online stores, more facts like other age lines and the city, the Solana Attestation Service, and a multi-party setup, audit and mainnet. Then AI agents: you delegate a scoped, revocable "verified human, 20 or over" credential to your agent; it attaches proof to x402 payments for age-restricted goods; and nullifiers let services cap how many agents one person runs. And global: ePassports follow the ICAO 9303 standard, with chip data signed by each country, so the same proof and the same Solana registry can work for travellers from any country.
