And it is about to grow. Governments now demand age and identity checks online: the UK since July 2025, Australia's minimum age of 16 for social media since December 2025, about half of US states, and in Japan remote KYC moves to the IC chip in April 2027. But most checks today still mean uploading a photo of your ID, and every upload is a stored copy. More checks times stored copies equals more breaches — at an average cost of 4.44 million dollars each.
