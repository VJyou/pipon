Stack: a Circom circuit with 1.49 million constraints and Groth16; on-device proving with mopro and rapidsnark; an Anchor program, zk_gate, on Solana; and a web verifier with snarkjs. There is no issuer server: the national certificate authority is the root of trust, and the phone does the proving.
