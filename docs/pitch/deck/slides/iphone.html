iPhone 15 Pro Max · SwiftUI + Core NFC + mopro (rapidsnark) · tested 2026-10-08 with the holder's own card; no personal data shown or stored

And this is not a mock-up of the future: the native iPhone app works today, and it is in TestFlight beta. You tap your My Number Card, the app reads the signing certificate over NFC and the chip signs the session challenge. The proof is made on the phone itself, in about nine and a half seconds. For a store, you scan the QR code and the store's browser verifies the proof in a third of a second. For a Web3 gate, a second claim by the same person from a new wallet is rejected on-chain.
