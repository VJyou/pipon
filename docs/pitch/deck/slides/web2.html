An online store that sells sake needs to know the buyer is 20 or over. No wallet, no signup: the store verifies the proof in the browser in about 200 milliseconds and checks the issuer CA against the trust list stored on Solana. It learns: 20 or over, the prefecture, and a pseudonymous ID for this store only. Point at "What it never sees".
