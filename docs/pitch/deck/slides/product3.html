How the Web3 product will work: you scan your card once. The proof "20 or over, one per person" is bound to your wallet; zk_gate verifies it on-chain and issues a non-transferable attestation to that wallet through the Solana Attestation Service, with an expiry. From then on, a service just asks your wallet to sign and reads the attestation on-chain: issued by zk_gate, not expired, held by this wallet. No card, no password. The attestation can't be sold or lent, it expires so you re-scan about once a year, the nullifier stops a second wallet for the same card, and you choose whether to use one wallet everywhere or separate ones to keep services apart.
