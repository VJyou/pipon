A precedent for the world

A national PKI on 100M+ cards and IC-chip KYC from 2027: a legal status for ZK proofs in Japan gives other countries a model — and speeds up blockchain adoption there.

Plan: study group from late 2026; association in early 2027; first output: a data-minimising age-check guideline for the April 2027 IC-chip shift.

Technology alone is not enough: a zero-knowledge proof has to be accepted as a lawful way to check someone's age or residence. That will not happen one company at a time. So p!pon builds the product as a company, and we are co-founding an industry association with other ZK identity projects, from any chain, plus accelerators and consultancies. It runs study sessions, writes guidelines, briefs regulators and seeks legal clarification together. This is good for everyone in Japan: once a ZK proof is lawful, no service needs a copy of your ID, so there is less to leak for all 124 million people. And it is a precedent for the world: Japan has a national PKI on over 100 million cards and moves remote KYC to the IC chip in 2027. If ZK proofs gain legal standing here, other countries get a model to follow, and blockchain adoption speeds up there too.
