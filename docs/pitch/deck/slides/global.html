Sources: TechCrunch (Sep 2026; 150M+ as reported, company confirmed licence and passport data), company disclosures and NHK (Japan), TechCrunch / NBC (Oct 2025), Reuters via Fortune (Jul 2025), Latitude Financial (Mar 2023).

This is not a Japanese problem. In September 2026 an ID-verification vendor in the US confirmed a breach reported at over 150 million records, driver's licences and passport numbers. The same month in Japan, a car-sharing service lost 1.6 million ID images. Last year a chat platform's age-check vendor leaked about 70,000 government ID photos, and a dating-safety app leaked 13,000 selfies and IDs uploaded for verification. In Australia, a lender lost 7.9 million driver's licence numbers. Every one of these was a copy someone was asked to upload.
