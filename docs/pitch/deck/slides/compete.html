Why not the free tools? Zero-knowledge ID tools like Self and ZKPassport are built on passports, and in Japan only about 18% of people hold a valid passport, while 84.5% hold a My Number Card. Passports also carry no address, so they cannot prove where you live; p!pon proves the prefecture today and the city next. And the lasting difference is Japan itself: we are built for the 2027 shift to IC-chip identity checks, with a path to certified revocation, and with the support, SDK and legal documents Japanese companies need to buy. Abroad we will meet these tools on the same passport standard, with a shared Solana registry on top.
