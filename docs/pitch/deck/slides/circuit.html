Inside the proof: four checks happen in private — the J-LIS CA signature over the certificate, the card's signature over a challenge bound to the wallet or browser session, the age, and the prefecture. This sample product reveals just two facts: age 20 or over, and the prefecture — plus technical values like the CA key hash and a per-service nullifier. That is only today's sample. The certificate also holds the name, date of birth, sex and full address, so the same pattern can prove other facts: other age lines, a city or ward, sex only where a service must know it, that a name matches an account without revealing it, or that the certificate is recent. Each service asks for exactly what it needs, nothing more.
